About this prototype and its data

This is a local concept, not a shipped Databricks feature. The layout, chart names, station identifiers, and headline values follow the bike-sharing demo we explored. $991, 200, and $4.95 are observed display values; the full-resolution underlying totals were not exported. Station and daily chart values here are illustrative, with synthetic revenue summing to $991. The member/casual series do not claim to reproduce the original rows. The scope cue, highlights, and explanations are proposed. The product navigation is visual context, not functional navigation. This uses the Scout theme, so chart and accent colors differ from the original screenshot.

The Dashboard page has five charts; only the daily chart responds to Rider Type. Build with Genie has one unconnected station chart. The selection and active chip persist across both pages, even where no chart is affected. This behavior was confirmed in the actual draft editor; the published viewer behavior has not been independently verified. This prototype illustrates the proposed viewer experience, not a pixel-perfect published-view replica.

All is the default. Coverage is computed for the current page. Switching pages preserves an open coverage overlay but closes chart-specific explanations. Clearing the filter clears it across both pages. No data is sent anywhere, no account is needed, and nothing is published. “Proposed scope cue” and “Show zero-coverage case” are reviewer controls, not proposed product UI.
